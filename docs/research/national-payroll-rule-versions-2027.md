# 2027 National Payroll Rule Versions for Appointed Personnel of civilian NGAs

**Research question:** Which national salary, compensation, contribution, tax, and deduction rules does a 2027 payroll for Appointed Personnel of a civilian National Government Agency need, and what is each rule's 2027 status: issued for 2027, continuing, or not yet issued?

**As of:** 2026-11-14 (Philippine Standard Time). Every source below was opened and read on that date unless the register says otherwise.

**Ticket and decision:** [GitHub issue #615](https://github.com/systemeror12/lingkod-hr/issues/615), resolving decision D15 of [the Phase 9 payroll spec (#12)](https://github.com/systemeror12/lingkod-hr/issues/12). This document adds no build work and blocks no build ticket by itself. A 2027 payroll period stays blocked until each rule it needs is recorded here as issued or continuing, or until it is confirmed not yet issued and blocking. The term "National Payroll Rule Version" follows [GLOSSARY.md](../../GLOSSARY.md).

## Source boundary

- **Rules covered.** Every component row, tax-treatment row, and "Effectivity and fail-closed controls" item in [native-payroll-component-authorities.md](native-payroll-component-authorities.md), plus the compensation, contribution, and deduction rows in [research/reference-agency-personnel-compensation-boundaries.md](../../research/reference-agency-personnel-compensation-boundaries.md). Civilian NGA appointed personnel only. JO/COS workers, special-regime populations, and the appointment rules of the 2025 ORAOHRA are out of scope: they decide who is covered, not a rate.
- **Owned elsewhere.** Terminal-leave valuation is researched in [leave-monetization-terminal-leave-valuation-authorities.md](leave-monetization-terminal-leave-valuation-authorities.md); this document records only the monetization rule's 2027 status. Partial-month salary is out of scope.
- **Authority.** Only the text of an issuance from its issuer, or a faithful full-text copy, is cited. Press reports, vendor and payroll-site pages, law-firm notes, and search snippets are pointers and are labelled as such. Where a source was a scan, it is marked read only where the page images were read (see the register).
- **Status.** Each row has exactly one status:
  - **Issued for 2027:** no row qualifies. No 2027 rule version was found issued.
  - **Continuing:** the authority on file has no expiry and stays in force in 2027 by its own words, quoted in the row.
  - **Not yet issued:** the 2027 value or condition depends on an issuance not yet made, and it stays blocking.
- **Annual GAA rule applied.** A rule whose amount, rate, or eligibility is set by the annual GAA is not continuing until the FY 2027 GAA is enacted.
- **Funding treated separately (judgment; see open question 2).** Several statutes route only *funding* to the annual GAA while fixing the amount or entitlement themselves. Those rules are recorded as continuing, and the funding condition is named in the row. The developer should confirm this reading.

## Resolution

- **Issued for 2027:** none. The search repeated on 2026-11-14 found no fourth-tranche implementing circular, no FY 2027 GAA, no 2027 PhilHealth rate, no 2027 GSIS AMC issuance, and no 2027 HDMF or ECC rate. The FY 2027 GAA bill (House Bill No. 10858) passed the House in October 2026 and had no Senate action in the search; that is a pointer, not a rule.
- **Continuing (15 rows):** pay-basis conversion, mid-year bonus core, year-end bonus core, cash gift, night-shift differential ceiling, overtime pay, leave monetization, absence and tardiness charging, GSIS rates, Pag-IBIG rate bands, ECC employer-only rule, compensation withholding, the de minimis ceilings (effectivity to confirm), the ₱90,000 aggregate exclusion, and the medical-allowance tax classification (superseded figure, open).
- **Not yet issued (12 rows, all blocking):** the salary fourth tranche, PERA, uniform and clothing allowance, medical allowance, RATA, mid-year bonus GAA-only conditions, retiree prorated bonus, GAA deduction payees and the ₱5,000 floor, GSIS AMC limit, PhilHealth premium for 2026 onward, Pag-IBIG Maximum Fund Salary, and the ECC contribution amount.

## Unresolved gaps and open questions for the developer

1. **Fourth-tranche use (R1).** EO 64 sets the fourth tranche effective 1 January 2027 and prints its table. The repo's native document says the fourth tranche "requires its applicable implementing circular/table before use". No DBM circular for 2027 was found on the DBM listing (S32) or in search. Decide whether the EO table may be used before a circular. This document does not decide it.
2. **Funding versus entitlement.** Several rules route only funding to the annual GAA (R5 medical, R7 mid-year, R9 year-end, R14 monetization funding, R12 night-shift funding). This document records the entitlement as continuing and the funding as a payroll condition. Confirm this reading before any payroll uses the continuing rows.
3. **GSIS premium base.** The repo's reference document says "9% member and 12% government on actual monthly salary". RA 8291 sec. 5(a) sets a band schedule around an AMC limit, with a 2% member share above it. Confirm the base the payroll applies, from GSIS billing, before R17 is used. The IRR's sec. 11 text was not read.
4. **ECC wording.** PD 626 art. 181(c) makes ECC employer-only. GAA sec. 61 mentions "government and employee shares" for ECC. This document follows PD 626 for R22 and treats the GAA sentence as generic wording.
5. **Night-shift implementing issuance.** The CSC notice (S31) says the IRR of RA 11701 was "published in two (2) newspapers of general circulation on 6 and 9 February 2023, and shall take effect on 24 February 2023". GAA sec. 69 (S3) makes the differential subject to "CSC-DBM-GCG J.C. No. 1 dated August 1, 2023". The joint circular itself was not opened: the CSC URL attempted returned a site template. Confirm which issuance governs.
6. **De minimis effectivity (R25).** The RR 29-2025 digest (S20) states no effectivity date. Confirm the date and the full text from the BIR issuances page.
7. **Medical de minimis figure (R27).** RMC 107-2024 uses ₱10,000 and RR 29-2025 uses ₱12,000. Confirm how the medical allowance is classified for FY 2027 after the change.
8. **Cash-gift tax treatment.** RR 29-2025 item (i) covers "Gifts given during Christmas", but the digest does not say whether a cash gift qualifies. A cash gift above that treatment falls into the ₱90,000 pool (R26). Open.
9. **Misprinted constant in a compilation (R14).** The re-typeset copy of CSC MC 2, s. 2016 in CSC's 2016 compilation (S29) prints "0.481927". The signed circular and Resolution No. 1501530, read from the [official scan](https://www.csc.gov.ph/phocadownload/userupload/irmo/mc/2016/MC%20No.%2002%20s.%202016.pdf) on 2026-11-14, print ".0481927", the value BC 2016-2 states. Nothing is open here; the compilation copy is not the source to load from.
10. **MC 41 effectivity.** The pages read (cover and sec. 64 to 67 page) show no separate effectivity sentence. The cover is dated 24 December 1998. Pages with secs. 1 to 10 and 35 to 63 were not read.
11. **GSIS AMC limit (R18) and ECC amount (R23).** Blocking; no current primary issuance located. See the blocking list.
