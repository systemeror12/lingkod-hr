# Personnel Action and Effect Matrix

**Research question:** Which personnel actions named by the BRD and workflow are supported for the Reference Agency Profile, what authority and evidence do they require, and what exact effective-dated changes may they make to Employee, Service Segment, Position Incumbency, Plantilla Position, salary grade, and salary step?

**As of:** 2026-08-31 (Philippine Standard Time)

**Profile:** civilian National Government Agency (NGA) personnel in first- and second-level career or non-career positions, with Plantilla-backed employment as the reference path. Casual and contractual engagement, plus Job Order (JO) and Contract of Service (COS) payment, remain separate configurable paths; presidential appointees, military/uniformed personnel, and special-law regimes are excluded unless separately configured. The governing external source is the **2025 Omnibus Rules on Appointments and Other Human Resource Actions (2025 ORAOHRA)**. It was promulgated as CSC Resolution No. 2500358 on 2025-04-30, published in *BusinessWorld* on 2025-07-18, and took effect on 2025-08-03. The 2025 ORAOHRA repeals or modifies the older CSC appointment/action issuances listed in Rule XV. [CSC, 2025 ORAOHRA, p. 17 (promulgation); p. 125 (publication/effectivity); Rule I §§1–3, p. 18; Rule XV §145, pp. 122–124; Rule XVII §§147–148, p. 125](https://www.csc.gov.ph/phocadownload/userupload/hrpso/2025-ORAOHRA/2025%20ORAOHRA.pdf)

## Decision

The Reference Agency Profile has eight source-mapped Phase 5 action contracts:

1. **same-item reinstatement** as a service-history restoration;
2. **separation**, but only through a typed canonical subtype and authoritative decision; retirement is enabled only after RA 8291/GSIS coverage and eligibility are verified;
3. **salary adjustment**;
4. **step increment**;
5. **reassignment** as a non-appointment duty/organizational assignment overlay;
6. **detail** as a non-appointment temporary cross-agency assignment overlay;
7. **designation** as a non-appointment additional-duty overlay; and
8. **secondment** as a non-appointment, MOA-backed temporary assignment overlay, only when the Reference Agency Profile has the required source-backed recipient/MOA boundary.

Source mapping does not mean effectuation-ready in the current schema: same-item reinstatement, typed non-retirement separation, salary adjustment, and step increment have complete primary-record semantics; retirement is conditional on verified RA 8291/GSIS coverage and eligibility; reassignment and designation need an approved assignment representation; and detail and secondment remain preparation/review-only until a typed overlay model exists.

The following actions remain valid government human resource actions, but their appointment behavior is owned by **#9** and must not be duplicated by Phase 5: original appointment, promotion, transfer, reappointment, reemployment, reinstatement to a comparable position, demotion to another position, reclassification, and change of status when it is the temporary-to-permanent or other appointment movement described by the ORAOHRA. Phase 5 may prepare a linked action record only if #9's contract explicitly supplies the issued appointment, assumption, authority, and effective facts. Phase 5 may not issue a synthetic appointment or activate a new appointee on its own.

`OTHER` is not an effect. Unsupported, special-law, agency-specific, or incompletely evidenced actions are rejected before approval and cannot fall through to a generic effect payload.

## Shared rules for every action

### 1. Action preparation is not effectuation

Preparation records intent and typed facts: Employee, canonical action type, proposed effectivity, current primary Service Segment, any target position or Organization Unit, reason, and exact authority evidence. Review and approval lock the approved version. Approval does not change Employee, Service Segment, Position Incumbency, Plantilla Position, salary grade, or salary step. An approved action remains pending until its effective date and all current validations pass.

Effectuation runs in one transaction. It locks the affected Employee, primary Service Segment, Position Incumbency, and any relevant Plantilla Positions; revalidates current state, Agency, authority, effective date, evidence, qualification, target availability, compensation alignment, and action-specific conditions; then calls the owning Plantilla/Personnel application services. A failed validation or write rolls back every effect. The idempotency key is tied to the approved action version and effectuation attempt; retry cannot create a second segment, incumbency, occupancy transition, or Audit Event.

### 2. Current projections are not historical truth

Employee is a stable Agency personnel identity. Current position, Organization Unit, salary grade, and salary step are projections from the applicable effective-dated Service Segment and Position Incumbency. They are not independently editable Employee facts. A successor Service Segment is required where a source-backed action changes a historical service fact; the prior segment remains closed and reconstructable. Primary Service Segments may not improperly overlap. Position Incumbencies may not overlap for the same Plantilla Position.

### 3. A Plantilla occupancy transition is narrow

`VACANT → OCCUPIED` is permitted only when the owning Plantilla service has an issued appointment plus the required assumption/effectivity facts, or when a source-backed action is explicitly defined to establish a successor occupancy. A reassignment, detail, designation, or secondment does **not** release the original item and does **not** occupy the target person's duty position. A temporary absence may allow a substitute appointment or designation under the external rules, but that is a separate action.

### 4. No direct status editing

`Employee.status` is a projection of a typed, evidenced event. The application does not expose arbitrary `employees.change_status` or `service_segments.status` writes. In the Reference Agency Profile, the supported projections are:

- **ACTIVE** while an employee has an effective primary service segment, including during reassignment, detail, designation, or secondment;
- **SEPARATED** for a valid non-death separation subtype; and
- **DECEASED** only for a death record supported by the required death evidence.

`ON_EXTENDED_LEAVE`, `SUSPENDED`, and similar states require their own authoritative leave or disciplinary contract. They are not silently created by `OTHER` or by an action that merely changes duty location.

### 5. Source date beats transaction date

The action's effective date is the legally or administratively effective date stated by the source instrument, not the date the HRIS user happens to enter it. The system must reject an effectivity earlier than the source authority, a signature, a required assumption, or a required start/termination date. For a successor segment beginning on date `d`, the predecessor closes on `d - 1` under the repository's inclusive-date convention. No effective-dated correction may erase a prior source, approval, or dependent record.

### 6. Roles and segregation of duties

The default Reference Agency workflow is:

| Stage | Default actor | Required control |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Prepare | Personnel Records Officer | Employee and all evidence are same-Agency; actor has `personnel.actions.create`; no effectuation. |
| Review/return/endorse | HRMO Reviewer or designated records reviewer | Check canonical action, source authority, QS/position/evidence, effectivity, and conflicts; preserve every return and revision. |
| Approve/reject | Appointing Authority or an authorized delegate | The authority instrument must cover the action, Agency, position/action class, and effective date. The preparer cannot approve their own action. |
| Effectuate | Personnel/Plantilla application service, invoked by an authorized effectuation worker or records officer | Re-lock and revalidate; do not trust the draft or approval snapshot. No direct cross-module table write. |
| Audit/read | Authorized auditor or read-only records role | Read-only access to sanitized history; no ability to alter an action or source document. |

The HRMPSB is assistorial for appointment selection under the approved Agency Merit Selection Plan; it does not replace the appointing authority. The 2025 ORAOHRA requires an approved Agency MSP and describes HRMPSB functions and the appointment process. Delegation is valid only when an effective Office Order, delegation instrument, or other authority-bearing record identifies the delegate and scope. A missing, expired, foreign, or out-of-scope delegation is a failed validation.

### Appointment-owned effect contract

These are linked consumption contracts, not Phase 5 appointment actions:

- **Original appointment:** #9 creates the Employee in `PREBOARDING`, changes it to `ACTIVE` after oath and assumption, starts the initial Service Segment and Position Incumbency on the actual assumption date, changes the target Plantilla item `RESERVED → OCCUPIED`, and supplies SG and step from the issued appointment.
- **Promotion, transfer, demotion to another position, comparable reinstatement, and reclassification:** #9 supplies the issued appointment and assumption. For the receiving Agency, Employee status remains or becomes `ACTIVE`; a source Agency transfer-out follows the explicit source-closure contract below. When the appointed position changes, #108 closes the predecessor segment and incumbency at `d - 1`, starts the successor at actual assumption `d`, releases the old item, and occupies the target only through Plantilla service; if the item does not change, occupancy remains continuous. SG and step come from the appointment, while any continuity or back-pay treatment comes from the source decision.
- **Reappointment:** #9 supplies the new appointment without a service gap. Employee remains `ACTIVE`; the predecessor Service Segment closes at `d - 1` and the successor starts on the applicable assumption/effective date `d`; the same-position Position Incumbency and Plantilla occupancy continue without a vacancy gap; appointment status, SG, and step come from the new appointment. A position change uses the movement contract above.
- **Appointment-style change of status:** #9 supplies the appointment for the temporary-to-permanent or career/non-career movement. Employee remains `ACTIVE`; a same-position successor Service Segment starts at `d` with the new appointment status; Position Incumbency, Plantilla occupancy, SG, and step remain unchanged unless the appointment states otherwise. No direct `Employee.status` write is allowed.
- **Reemployment:** #9 supplies the new appointment after a service gap. Employee changes from `SEPARATED` to `ACTIVE` only on actual assumption; the prior service remains closed, and the successor Service Segment and Position Incumbency start on assumption. Any target occupancy is performed only through Plantilla service.

### Appointment effectivity and assumption contract

The ORAOHRA states that an appointment takes effect on the date signed by the appointing authority, subject to the applicable rules. The actual assumption date is the basis for salary payment and service rendered; for a transferee, it marks the first working day in the new agency. An appointment cannot be used to claim a Phase 5 service effect without the #9 appointment and assumption records. [O, Rule VI §§17–23, pp. 65–69]

The BRD's rule that service, occupancy, and payroll scope close “on the effective date” describes the business transition. The `d - 1` values below are inclusive historical end dates; Employee status and Plantilla state change on `d`, the appointment record remains #9-owned, and payroll scope closes on `d` for separation or follows the receiving service on actual assumption `d` for a movement. Finalized payroll snapshots are not rewritten.

For a promotion, transfer, demotion, reappointment, reemployment, comparable reinstatement, or reclassification, #9 must return at least:

- exact issued appointment version and nature;
- appointing authority identity and effective delegation, if any;
- target Agency, Plantilla Position, position version, Organization Unit, QS, salary grade, salary step, and appointment status;
- appointment signature/effectivity date;
- oath and actual assumption when the new service may start;
- CSC submission/approval or validation state where applicable;
- authority documents, qualification evidence, publication/selection evidence, clearance, and any required exception; and
- an idempotency/correlation reference that lets Phase 5 prove it consumed the same appointment once.

Without that contract, #108 must fail closed rather than create a target occupancy or a second appointment record.

## Phase 5 effect matrix

The following rows are the exact application contract for supported Phase 5 actions. “No change” means no new authoritative row or state transition; it does not permit a hidden direct edit. An overlay is a separate typed event/record that does not become a primary Service Segment or Plantilla occupancy. The current schema has no adequate cross-Agency overlay table; detail and secondment therefore remain blocked until the owning model can store the required assignment facts without foreign Agency FKs.

The matrix uses inclusive date ranges. A business effect begins on date `d`; a predecessor Service Segment or Position Incumbency therefore stores `effective_to = d - 1`, while the Plantilla state transition is effective on `d`. “Close on `d - 1`” is storage encoding, not a one-day delay in the action.

### Position-movement and assignment semantics

For every separation row below, `OCCUPIED → VACANT` applies only when the source appointment actually occupies a Plantilla item. A non-Plantilla casual or contractual record, JO, or COS engagement has no Plantilla transition and instead uses its configured engagement/payment closure. [H, docs/mvp-brd.md §§4.1–4.2, 27.1–27.3]

For those non-Plantilla engagement records, the Employee, primary Service Segment, Position Incumbency, salary grade, and salary step effects in the matrix are not authoritative; #109 must keep separation effectuation blocked until the configured engagement/payment contract supplies them.

### Service-status semantics

The BRD lists “transfer to another agency” as a separation type. The source-backed contract treats the government-service move as the #9-owned `TRANSFER` appointment above, but closes the source Agency record as a typed transfer-out event: source Employee `ACTIVE → SEPARATED` on `d` after clearance; source Service Segment and Position Incumbency end at `d - 1`; source Plantilla item and payroll scope close on `d`; and source `AGENCY_MEMBERSHIP` deactivates with active sessions revoked according to the approved risk-based access schedule. The receiving Agency creates or activates its own Agency-owned Employee through #9, starts successor service on actual assumption `d`, occupies its target through Plantilla service, and takes SG/step from the receiving appointment. Reuse of `person_key` is allowed only through the identity contract; no cross-Agency foreign record is created. Government-service continuity is preserved by linked transfer appointment/history, not by keeping the source Employee `ACTIVE`. #108 owns the appointment/position effect; #109 consumes the source appointment-closure acknowledgment and owns clearance and access closure. [O, Rule IV §11(c); Rule VI §23; H]

#### Salary schedule and action boundary

The current payroll research treats basic salary as a projection from the authorized appointment/position, SG, step, service fraction, and effective salary table. A national pay-level tranche can change the amount paid without changing the employee's SG/step identity. An action may not carry an arbitrary peso amount, free-form grade/step, or undocumented retroactive date. If an agency authority selects a value or changes a position's title/rank/QS, the input belongs to the applicable authority-bearing configuration or appointment/reclassification path, not `SALARY_ADJUSTMENT`.

## Correction, cancellation, supersession, and invalidation

These are lifecycle operations on an action and its dependent effects. They are not destructive edits.

### Correction

- A draft may be corrected by its authorized preparer before submission; revisions and reasons remain visible.
- A submitted action may be returned with a required deficiency comment. Resubmission creates a new draft revision while preserving the prior review history.
- An approved, effective, or source-bearing action is immutable. A factual error, wrong effective date, wrong target, wrong authority reference, or wrong effect uses a reasoned successor/correction action linked to the original. The successor must re-enter review and approval when the correction changes a material fact.
- A correction must preserve the original action, source authority, approval actors/dates, effective history, dependent Service Segments, Position Incumbencies, Plantilla transitions, Audit Events, outbox/correlation records, and issued Service Record snapshots. Correcting source history never rewrites an issued snapshot; generate a linked replacement snapshot.

### Cancellation

- A draft may be cancelled by the preparer while it has no effect.
- An approved but not-yet-effective action may be cancelled only by the approving authority or an in-scope delegate, with a lawful reason. It remains an immutable approved/cancelled history item and produces no service, position, or compensation effect.
- An effective action is not “cancelled” retroactively. Use correction, prospective supersession, or authoritative invalidation. If an appointment action is cancelled under the ORAOHRA, #9 owns the appointment cancellation and its Plantilla consequences. [O, Rule XI §§107–110]

### Supersession

An effective action is superseded only by a new, valid, approved action whose source explicitly corrects or replaces the future fact. The new action links `supersedes_action_id`, starts on its own effective date, and closes the prior future projection without deleting the prior action. A supersession cannot manufacture a retroactive gap, erase authority, or silently alter a finalized payroll or Service Record snapshot.

### Invalidation and reversal

CSC disapproval/invalidation, a court/CSC/agency reversal, or a disapproved secondment MOA is not equivalent to user cancellation. Store the exact decision, receipt date, finality/appeal state, and scope. For appointment invalidation, the ORAOHRA provides the 15-day post-receipt termination rule unless a timely motion/appeal applies, and preserves de facto-officer treatment for valid public reliance; do not delete the appointment or its service history. [O, Rule XI §§107–110] A reversal that restores an employee is a new authoritative event (for example, same-item reinstatement), not a hidden undo.

Dependent effects are handled through the owning service in dependency order: action lifecycle → Personnel service facts → Position Incumbency → Plantilla occupancy → projections → outbox/Audit. If any dependent service rejects the operation, the transaction rolls back. Existing finalized payroll, audit, evidence, and printable Service Record snapshots remain retained and linked.
