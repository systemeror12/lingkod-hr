# Official personnel and compensation boundaries for the Reference Agency Profile

**Research question:** Which current official sources constrain civilian National Government Agency (NGA) personnel categories, Contract of Service (COS) and Job Order (JO) treatment, and salary, allowance, contribution, tax, and deduction baselines relevant to the Reference Agency Profile?

**As of:** 2026-08-09 (Philippine Standard Time)

## Executive findings

1. A civilian NGA must not collapse appointed personnel and COS/JO workers into one employment category. Appointed personnel are governed by the civil-service career/non-career framework and the employment statuses in the 2025 Omnibus Rules on Appointments and Other Human Resource Actions (2025 ORAOHRA). COS/JO workers under the current joint circular have no employer-employee relationship with the agency, are outside civil-service laws, and are paid from Maintenance and Other Operating Expenses (MOOE), not Personnel Services (PS).
2. The current 2026 salary baseline for covered NGA appointees is the **third tranche** of Executive Order (EO) No. 64, s. 2024, implemented by Department of Budget and Management (DBM) National Budget Circular (NBC) No. 601 dated 22 January 2026. It is a national salary-grade/step schedule, not an agency-created pay table. EO No. 64 expressly excludes JO, COS, consultancy, and service-contract engagements without an employer-employee relationship.
3. The FY 2026 General Appropriations Act (GAA), Republic Act (RA) No. 12314, supplies the current annual caps, coverage, priority, and conditions for common NGA allowances and payroll deductions. These provisions are time-bound and must not be treated as perpetual constants.
4. GSIS, PhilHealth, and Pag-IBIG/HDMF rules apply to appointed personnel through compulsory employee coverage. COS/JO workers instead fall outside GSIS where there is no employer-employee relationship; the 2025 COS/JO circular treats SSS, PhilHealth, and Pag-IBIG participation as voluntary or self-employed membership supported through an agency-provided contract premium of up to 20%, subject to funding availability.
5. BIR withholding on compensation and BIR treatment of individual JO/service contractors are different regimes. A label such as "JO" or "COS" is not enough to select a withholding rate: the payee's taxpayer category, VAT status, gross-sales threshold, sworn declaration, and tax option matter.

## Personnel categories that must remain distinct

### Appointed personnel

EO No. 292, Book V, Title I-A, Chapter 2 establishes career and non-career service. The more operational 2025 ORAOHRA, Rule IV, sec. 9, then identifies these employment statuses:

- permanent;
- temporary;
- substitute;
- coterminous;
- fixed term;
- contractual;
- casual.

Rule IV, sec. 10 separately defines provisional appointments for teachers; it is not a generic eighth status under sec. 9.

The list matters because **contractual appointment is not Contract of Service**. The former is an appointment and, under 2025 ORAOHRA sec. 9(f)-(g), contractual and casual appointees receive the benefits of regular employees. The latter is a procurement/service engagement without an employer-employee relationship under CSC-COA-DBM JC No. 1, s. 2025.

The 2025 ORAOHRA was promulgated as CSC Resolution No. 2500358 on 30 April 2025, published on 18 July 2025, and states that it took effect on 3 August 2025 ([CSC official PDF, p. 91](https://csc.gov.ph/phocadownload/userupload/hrpso/2025-ORAOHRA/2500358%20ORAOHRA%202025.pdf)). It is therefore the current appointment rule set for this research date.

### COS and JO workers

CSC-COA-DBM JC No. 1, s. 2025 is the current cross-agency source located for FY 2026. Its official DBM FAQ confirms that agencies may hire or renew COS/JO workers in FY 2026 under that circular but may not exceed the baseline number whose contracts were valid through 31 December 2025 ([FAQ, question 1](https://www.dbm.gov.ph/images/pdffiles/FAQs-on-JO-and-COS-Workers.pdf)).

The circular's operative distinctions are:

- **Individual COS:** consultant, learning-service provider, or technical expert for a special project/job, not a substitute for the agency's regular functions; maximum one-year contract, renewable at the agency head's option but not beyond that official's term (secs. 7.2 and 9.1).
- **JO:** a specific piece of work or short emergency/intermittent/manual/trades-and-crafts service outside regular functions (secs. 6.4, 8 and 9.2).
- **Institutional COS:** procurement from a registered service provider; workers remain employees of that provider, which bears labor-law compensation and benefit obligations (sec. 7.1).

The 20% figure is a **maximum premium**, not a guaranteed fixed rate: sec. 10.3 says the agency shall provide a premium not exceeding 20%, subject to funding availability. Likewise, overtime pay, travel reimbursement, flexible work arrangements, and any additional ancillary benefit in sec. 11 remain subject to the cited rules, availability of funds, and contract/internal guidelines.

## Currency and applicability limits

- **Annual rules:** The allowance, bonus, deduction-priority, and PHP 5,000 net-take-home figures above come from the **FY 2026** GAA. They require a new currency check for another fiscal year.
- **Salary tranche:** The 2026 third tranche is current only through 31 December 2026 under EO No. 64's schedule. The fourth tranche begins 1 January 2027.
- **JO/COS joint-circular effectivity record:** The signed JC says it takes effect 15 days after newspaper publication and filing with the Office of the National Administrative Register. The official DBM issuance page and FY 2026 FAQ treat it as operative, but the official pages reviewed did not state the newspaper-publication and ONAR filing dates. Do not infer a precise legal effectivity date from the 15 December 2025 signature date alone.
- **PhilHealth:** Advisory No. 2025-0002 is the latest rate notice located. The [PhilHealth 2026 circular archive](https://www.philhealth.gov.ph/circulars/2026/) contained no replacement contribution-rate circular through the research cutoff, but payroll should still validate the current EPRS-generated amount before remittance.
- **GSIS:** RA No. 8291 remains the coverage and contribution base and permits the GSIS Board to readjust contribution rates. The agency's current GSIS billing/remittance record is the safer source for the exact assessed amount than an uncaveated hard-coded legacy AMC limit.
- **BIR JO/COS administration:** RMC No. 51-2018 remains the JO/service-contract-specific classification source located, but parts of its registration and invoicing checklist predate RA No. 11976 (Ease of Paying Taxes Act). RR No. 3-2024 now uses accrual/billing concepts and a single **invoice** for goods and services ([BIR RR No. 3-2024](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RR%203-2024%20%28final%29.pdf)); current BIR registration and invoicing rules must be applied with RMC No. 51-2018 rather than copying its 2018 checklist verbatim.
- **Allowance tax treatment:** The compensation authority and tax treatment are separate checks. BIR RR No. 4-2025 includes uniform/clothing allowance up to PHP 7,000 per year among de minimis benefits ([BIR PDF](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RR%204-2025.pdf)). BIR RMC No. 107-2024 discusses HMO premiums up to PHP 10,000 as de minimis ([BIR digest](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RMC%20No.%20107-2024%20Digest.pdf)); applicability to a government medical-allowance payment still depends on the grant mode and facts rather than the DBM label alone.
- **Special populations:** This note covers a generic civilian NGA. Teachers, public health workers, scientists, foreign-service personnel, members of the Judiciary/Constitutional Commissions, GOCC personnel under separate compensation systems, military/uniformed personnel, and workers covered by special laws need their own applicability review.
- **Agency-specific grants:** "Up to," "subject to funding," rank-based, performance-based, or contract-based amounts are not universal defaults. The authorizing issuance, appropriation, appointment/contract, and internal guideline together determine an actual payment.

## Source hierarchy for later validation

When two stored values conflict, validate in this order:

1. current statute, annual GAA, or executive issuance;
2. current joint/implementing circular from the responsible oversight or contribution agency;
3. authorized DBM staffing/position record, CSC-approved/validated appointment, or signed COS/JO contract and internal guideline;
4. current member/taxpayer registration and issuer-generated billing/remittance record; and
5. agency-entered profile value.

This is a hierarchy of authority, not a product-scope recommendation.
