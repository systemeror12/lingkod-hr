# PERA authorities

**Research question:** What do the FY 2026 GAA and the DBM PERA circulars say about who receives the Personnel Economic Relief Allowance, how much, and how it changes for part-time service, days without pay, a partial month, details, and exclusions?

**As of:** 2026-11-15 (Philippine Standard Time)

**Scope:** Civilian Appointed Personnel in a National Government Agency, as in [native-payroll-component-authorities.md](native-payroll-component-authorities.md). LGUs, GOCCs, GFIs, military and uniformed personnel, and special-regime populations are out of scope. This record states what the sources say and which product rule #342 takes from each. It answers [#342][i342].

## What #342 takes from each

- **Amount.** The PERA rule version holds the monthly amount of P1 and P4 and the daily amount of P5. P5 states the daily amount rounded to ₱90.91, so the version carries it as written. Each fiscal year's GAA restates the amount, so the version ends on 31 December and needs a successor ([national-payroll-rule-versions-2027.md](national-payroll-rule-versions-2027.md) R3).
- **Full month.** A full month of monthly full-time service pays the monthly amount (P4).
- **Days without pay.** A month with days without pay, from leave without pay or whole-day absence, pays the daily amount for each day with basic pay (P5). The days with basic pay are `22 − days without pay`, the same count that CSC MC No. 8, s. 2014 uses for basic salary. Under P3, a month with no day of basic pay pays no PERA.
- **Part-time.** A full month of part-time service pays the monthly amount times the employment fraction (P7).
- **Detail.** The Employee's own Agency pays PERA (P8). An Agency's payroll covers its own Employees, so nothing changes.
- **Agency Payroll Applicability.** P4 pays PERA to NGA personnel with no Agency decision. PERA is an unconditional component, and no applicability record is needed.
- **Tax.** PERA is not subject to income tax or withholding ([native-payroll-component-authorities.md](native-payroll-component-authorities.md), tax table; RR No. 11-2018 sec. 2.79(B)).

## Open questions

1. **Partial month of service.** P4 grants the monthly amount for full-time service of 22 working days a month. P5 reduces it for absences and leave without pay only. No item covers an Employee who assumes duty or separates inside the month, and P10 sends uncovered cases to the DBM. The case blocks, as basic salary does ([partial-month-basic-salary-authorities.md](partial-month-basic-salary-authorities.md)).
2. **Part-time with days without pay.** P5 and P7 each set one case. Neither says how they combine. The case blocks.
3. **Casual daily wage.** P6 sets ₱90.91 a day, but no recorded rule fixes how many days a casual daily wage pays in a month, as for basic salary. The case blocks.
4. **Days without pay in an earlier month.** Under ADR 0014 a late day without pay is deducted in a later payroll. P5 computes that earlier month's PERA from all its days with basic pay, and P4 and P5 give different amounts (22 × ₱90.91 is ₱2,000.02). The difference needs the days the earlier payroll already counted, which the calculation does not read. The case blocks until that count is read.
5. **Exclusions without a recorded fact.** P1's station abroad and P11 items 4.1 to 4.3 and 4.5 turn on facts Personnel does not record: a station abroad with overseas allowances, a constitutional no-emolument position, an agency with its own compensation system, and barangay service. These populations are outside the Reference Agency Profile ([research/reference-agency-personnel-compensation-boundaries.md](../../research/reference-agency-personnel-compensation-boundaries.md), Special populations). P11 item 4.4 workers are not Appointed Personnel and are never on payroll (#27). Until Personnel records station abroad, an Employee stationed abroad would receive a PERA line.
