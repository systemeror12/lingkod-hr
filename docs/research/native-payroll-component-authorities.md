# Official authorities for native Appointed Personnel payroll components

**Research question:** Which current official Philippine sources fully determine the eligibility, calculation, tax treatment, and effectivity of the agreed Native Payroll Components for generic civilian National Government Agency (NGA) Appointed Personnel, and which components still require an Authorized Payroll Component Input?

**As of:** 2026-08-10 (Philippine Standard Time)

## Decision rule

A component is **native** only when current national authorities supply its eligibility boundary, calculation basis, numeric rate or table, and tax treatment. Employee- and agency-specific facts expressly contemplated by those rules—appointment, grade and step, approved attendance, position rank, official vehicle assignment, performance rating, or approved overtime/leave quantity—remain authoritative source facts and do not make the national calculation incomplete.

A component or variant is an **Authorized Payroll Component Input** when national authority supplies only a ceiling or permission and leaves the actual rate, grant mode, reimbursable amount, or entitlement decision to an agency issuance or approval. The input must carry that authority and approval; no product default may fill the gap.

This applies the personnel boundary established by [Research official personnel and compensation boundaries for the Reference Agency Profile](https://github.com/systemeror12/lingkod-hr/issues/17) and tests the components selected in [Define the Reference Agency Profile payroll boundary](https://github.com/systemeror12/lingkod-hr/issues/27). It covers appointed civilian NGA personnel only, not Job Order/Contract of Service workers or special-regime populations.

## Resolution

### Complete native calculation rules

The following have a complete generic civilian NGA calculation rule, subject to effective-dated authoritative facts:

- basic salary, including the monthly full-time rate, proportional part-time rate, and casual daily wage;
- salary effects of absence without pay, with tardiness and undertime first charged under the government leave rules;
- PERA;
- uniform/clothing allowance for the cash-payroll variant after the agency records qualification, grant form, and payment schedule;
- commutable RATA for eligible incumbents, including current rank rates, actual-work schedules, and the official-vehicle restriction;
- mid-year bonus;
- year-end bonus;
- cash gift;
- overtime pay from approved overtime quantities after the agency has lawfully chosen cash rather than compensatory time off;
- leave monetization from an approved number of days; and
- ordinary employee compensation withholding tax, including the component classifications below.

### Authorized inputs or fail-closed variants

- **Medical allowance:** the national rules determine coverage, the annual ceiling, part-time proportionality, exclusions, six-month service condition, and tax exemption, but DBM Budget Circular No. 2024-6 allows several grant modes and requires each agency to issue internal procedures governing release, use, and monitoring. A cash payroll line therefore requires an Authorized Payroll Component Input identifying the agency issuance, grant mode, amount/schedule, employee qualification, and supporting proof. A procured group HMO is not an employee payroll earning.
- **Night-shift differential:** Republic Act No. 11701 and the current FY 2026 GAA authorize a rate **not exceeding 20%** of hourly basic pay, as determined by the agency head. National law does not select one generic percentage. The approved night hours can be calculated natively, but the percentage and its agency authority must be an effective-dated Authorized Payroll Component Input.
- **Reimbursable RATA variants:** National Budget Circular No. 593 fully determines eligibility and ceilings but makes some OIC, scholarship/training, and comparable variants reimbursable or dependent on a designation/certification. The standard commutable-incumbent path is native; an actual reimbursable amount must enter through an approved, authority-bearing input and may not exceed the native ceiling.

The medical-allowance and night-shift-differential findings narrow the unqualified Native Payroll Component list in the closed payroll-boundary decision. Keeping either as a calculated line without the required agency authority would invent a public-sector rule.

| Cash gift | The FY 2026 PHP 5,000 gift is within the current PHP 6,000 de minimis ceiling for Christmas/major-anniversary gifts. | BIR RR No. 29-2025, sec. 2.78.1(A)(3)(i). |

The payroll tax engine must apply ordinary periodic and supplementary withholding, the PHP 90,000 annual aggregate, current de minimis ceilings, and year-end adjustment as versioned rules. A benefit's compensation authority does not by itself establish a tax exemption.

## Effectivity and fail-closed controls

- **Annual currency:** PERA, uniform/clothing allowance, medical allowance, RATA, bonuses, cash gift, and related conditions or amounts must be revalidated against each fiscal year's GAA.
- **Salary tranche:** the stored 2026 rule must expire on 2026-12-31; EO No. 64's fourth tranche starts 2027-01-01 and requires its applicable implementing circular/table before use. The 2027 status of each rule, as checked on 2026-11-14, is in [national-payroll-rule-versions-2027.md](national-payroll-rule-versions-2027.md).
- **Partial month of service:** no national rule is recorded for the basic salary of a monthly-paid Employee who assumes duty or separates inside a month. The case blocks ([partial-month-basic-salary-authorities.md](partial-month-basic-salary-authorities.md)).
- **Agency-selected values:** a night-shift percentage, medical cash mode/schedule, and reimbursable RATA amount require an effective-dated agency authority and approval. Cash OT over CTO is not a standing agency value: the agency head chooses it on each overtime authority, and only where CTO for all the hours would adversely affect operations ([overtime-coc-cto-authorities.md](overtime-coc-cto-authorities.md#choosing-compensation)). Missing evidence blocks the affected line.
- **Source quantities:** locked DTR/leave data, approved night/OT hours, approved monetization days, official-vehicle use, performance rating, service dates, and designation records are authoritative inputs. The national calculator must not manufacture them.
- **Tax versions:** BIR RR No. 29-2025 is the latest de minimis amendment located as of the research date, while RR No. 11-2018 and Annex E remain the compensation-withholding framework. Store the issuer, verification date, legal effectivity, and supersession rather than assuming an issuance date is its effectivity date.
- **Special populations:** teachers, health workers, scientists, foreign-service personnel, Judiciary/Constitutional offices with fiscal autonomy, GOCCs under another compensation system, and military/uniformed personnel require a separate applicability decision. An unsupported category-specific rule blocks processing.

## Implementation consequence

The smallest lawful boundary is one native calculator per fully determined rule above, plus the existing authority-bearing payroll input path for the three identified discretionary variants. No free-form formula, agency override of a national table, default 20% night rate, or automatic PHP 7,000 medical cash earning is supported by these sources.
