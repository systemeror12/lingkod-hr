# Uniform/clothing allowance authorities

**Research question:** What do the FY 2026 GAA and DBM Budget Circular No. 2024-1 say about who receives the uniform/clothing allowance (U/CA) in cash, how much, and how the six-month service rule, part-time service, and transfers change it?

**As of:** 2026-11-15 (Philippine Standard Time)

**Scope:** Civilian Appointed Personnel in a National Government Agency, as in [native-payroll-component-authorities.md](native-payroll-component-authorities.md). LGUs, GOCCs, military and uniformed personnel, foreign-service personnel stationed abroad, and special-regime populations are out of scope. This record states what the sources say and which product rule #631 takes from each. It answers [#631][i631].

## What #631 takes from each

- **Cash grant only.** U6 allows three forms, and U14 leaves the choice and the payment schedule to the Agency's internal guidelines. Only the cash form of U6 item 7.1.3 is a payroll earning. A procured uniform (7.1.1) is procurement, not payroll. The cash grant is the conditional `CLOTHING_ALLOWANCE` component. The Agency records it as an Agency Payroll Applicability whose authority Document Version is its internal guideline: the grant form, who qualifies, and the payment schedule. The record covers the whole Agency or one named Employee, and the Employee's own record is preferred. Without a record in effect on the payment date, no line is written. An Agency that grants the 7.1.2 form records no Applicability, so no line is written.
- **Payment schedule.** The Agency pays the U/CA through a special Payroll Period that names `CLOTHING_ALLOWANCE` ([ADR 0015](../adr/0015-regular-payroll-period-is-one-calendar-month.md)). The period covers its fiscal year, 1 January to 31 December, because U7 reckons service over "a particular fiscal year". Its payment date is the Agency's scheduled payment.
- **Amount.** The rule version holds the annual amount for full-time service. U1 and U3 cap it at ₱7,000 for FY 2026. U7 grants "the full rates", U5 allows a lower uniform rate only for GOCC and LGU funds, and U13 funds an NGA for ₱7,000 per employee. For an NGA, the full rate is therefore the GAA amount. Each fiscal year's GAA restates it, so the version ends on 31 December and needs a successor ([national-payroll-rule-versions-2027.md](national-payroll-rule-versions-2027.md) R4).
- **Six-month service.** U7 and U9 require six months of service in the fiscal year. U17 counts six months as 180 days under EO No. 292, and counts days of service as calendar days. The rule version holds the 180 days. The count is the calendar days of the Employee's Service Segments in the fiscal year, including dates after the payment date, because U7 says "are to render". U17 and U18 example 3 count service still to come in the same way. Paid leave is inside a Service Segment, so it counts, as U7 requires. At 180 days or more the rule is met. Under 180 days, no line is written (U9; U18 examples 1 and 5). An Employee who leaves before 31 December after at least 180 days has rendered the six months and is paid.
- **Already in service.** An Employee in the Agency's service on 1 January has been in government service since before the fiscal year, as in U18 example 4. A promotion or step increment does not interrupt service and is not a new entry.
- **Newly hired.** Service that starts after 1 January under an `ORIGINAL` appointment is a new hire under U9. Its days count toward the 180.
- **Part-time.** U4 makes the part-time U/CA the full-time amount times hours of part-time service a day over 8. Personnel records part-time service as an employment fraction, the share of full-time service, so the amount is the annual amount times that fraction, as #342 does for PERA.
- **Detail.** The mother Agency pays (U10). An Agency's payroll covers its own Employees, so nothing changes.
- **Tax.** The line is a non-taxable de minimis benefit: ₱7,000 is within the ₱8,000 ceiling of U19.

## Cases that block

Each of these writes no line and raises a blocking exception, because no item settles it from what LingkodHR records (U16):

1. **Entry by transfer.** Service that starts after 1 January under a `TRANSFER` appointment falls under U11. Whether the former agency granted U/CA this year decides 9.3.1 against 9.3.2, and 9.3.3 needs the new agency head's approval. LingkodHR records neither the certification nor the approval.
2. **Any other mid-year entry.** Service that starts after 1 January under an appointment that is neither `ORIGINAL` nor `TRANSFER`, or with no appointment on record, such as reemployment or a migrated baseline. U7 counts government service, not service in this Agency, and the government service rendered earlier in the year, and any grant made with it, is not recorded.
3. **A period that is not one fiscal year.** U7 reckons service over a fiscal year, so a special period that pays the U/CA must cover 1 January to 31 December of one year.
4. **No rule version in effect on every date of the fiscal year, or a version that changes inside it.**
5. **A casual daily wage.** U2 covers casual personnel, and U3 and U4 set the amount by full-time or part-time hours, but Personnel records no daily hours for a casual daily wage.
6. **A pay basis or employment fraction that changes inside the year.** U4 sets one proportion, and no item apportions a year of mixed full-time and part-time service.
7. **No pay basis recorded,** or part-time service with no employment fraction.
8. **An amount that is not whole centavos.** No recorded rule rounds the U/CA.

## Open questions

1. **Days that are not service.** U7 counts paid leave as service, and U17 assumes no leave without pay. U12 and U18 example 6 exclude study leave and study, training, or scholarship grants. Leave without pay and these grants are recorded in Leave and Attendance, which a special period does not read. Like the year-end bonus calculator, this calculator counts Service Segment days. It would pay an Employee whose days without pay or study leave bring the year under 180 days. Until Payroll reads those days for the fiscal year, the developer decides whether that case should block.
2. **Always-uniformed personnel.** U8 waives the six-month rule for personnel required to wear uniforms at all times, and U18 example 2 grants a retiring nurse. Personnel records no such duty, so an Employee under 180 days gets no line. This under-grants. It does not over-grant.
3. **One grant a year.** U7 grants the U/CA once a fiscal year. Nothing stops an Agency from creating two special periods for the same year, as with the year-end bonus. Review before certification is the control.
4. **2027 amount.** No FY 2027 GAA is signed. The 2027 version waits for it ([national-payroll-rule-versions-2027.md](national-payroll-rule-versions-2027.md) R4).
