# Official authorities for Attendance Work Schedule calculations

**Research question:** Which current primary Philippine government authorities govern ordinary work hours, flexible and compressed schedules, shifting and overnight duty, breaks, rest days, holidays, calculation units, grace periods, and rounding for civilian National Government Agency (NGA) Appointed Personnel? Which rules are mandatory national constraints, which require agency authorization or evidence-backed configuration, and which remain product choices?

**As of:** 2026-09-15 (Philippine Standard Time)

**Scope:** The Reference Agency's civilian NGA Appointed Personnel. Job Order/Contract of Service workers, military and uniformed personnel, teachers, and other special-law populations are not silently included. A special regime needs its own authority and applicability decision.

## Decision

The sources establish a minimum-hours and service-continuity framework, not a canonical timestamp-matching algorithm or a legal definition of `Work Date`.

For the ordinary Reference Agency path:

- The national floor is eight hours per day, five days per week or 40 hours per week, exclusive of lunch. The default office schedule is 8:00 a.m.–12:00 noon and 1:00–5:00 p.m. on days other than Saturdays, Sundays, and holidays. An approved agency schedule can replace that default within the authorities below.
- A Work Schedule Version should be an immutable, effective-dated, authority-bearing definition. A Schedule Assignment should separately bind one Appointed Personnel record to one version for an effective range. This preserves historical Attendance Days when a schedule changes.
- A duty interval may cross midnight. Storing `start_time`, `end_time`, and an explicit `end_next_calendar_day` flag is a product representation of the source-backed fact that the duty can run into the next day; there is no need for a separate night-shift calculation engine. The product rule that a cross-midnight interval's `work_date` is the local date on which it begins is a project choice and must be documented as such.
- Breaks are separate intervals excluded from expected duty minutes when the approved schedule says they are unpaid. A break may also cross midnight. The sources do not prescribe one universal break length for every agency schedule.
- Store and calculate timestamps in exact whole minutes. Do not add a national five-, ten-, or fifteen-minute grace period or quarter-hour rounding rule: no authoritative source located supplies one, and the Civil Service Commission's attendance rules expressly treat tardiness and undertime as relevant regardless of the number of minutes. The whole-minute representation and any display rounding are project choices, not national mandates.
- Attendance recording and calculation do not need a per-day approval layer. The source-required authority is for the agency's work arrangement, internal rules, and schedule assignment. Attendance Correction can own correction approval; a raw event or calculated Attendance Day should not wait for a second approval merely because it is associated with a schedule.

## Findings by rule

### Ordinary hours and attendance records

Rule XVII is the national baseline for the Reference Agency. The 8-hour/40-hour requirement is exclusive of lunch, and the default 8:00–12:00 / 1:00–5:00 schedule is stated for ordinary days other than Saturday, Sunday, and holidays. The same rule requires daily attendance records and strict office-hours enforcement. The rule does not define a `Work Date`, a timestamp-matching tolerance, or a rounding unit. [CSC MC No. 1, s. 2017](https://www.csc.gov.ph/downloads/memorandum-circulars/category/45-2017?download=355%3Amc-no-01-s-2017)

An appointed employee may therefore have an effective schedule different from the baseline only when the agency has the applicable authority and records the arrangement. The model should distinguish the national floor from the evidence that activates a local schedule.

### Flexible, compressed, and shifting work

Flexible hours remain a 40-hour, 5-day week and must preserve continuous frontline service. CWW and work shifting are permitted only within the conditions and internal-guideline requirements in CSC MC No. 6, s. 2022. Work shifting is not a general permission for every office to create an overnight roster; its core case is an agency legally required to operate continuously, plus the specified security, safety, health, and emergency situations.

The 2026 CSC amendment and OP MC No. 114 add current energy-conservation arrangements. They are especially important for effective dating: a current four-day CWW example may have 10 duty hours per day exclusive of a one-hour lunch, while another agency may adopt a common WFH day or a different permitted pattern. A calculator must resolve the schedule version and the applicable holiday/suspension rule rather than assume that every 4-day arrangement is the same. [CSC MC No. 6, s. 2022](https://csc.gov.ph/phocadownload/userupload/irmo/mc/2022/MC%20No.%2006,%20s.%202022.pdf), [CSC MC No. 6, s. 2026](https://csc.gov.ph/phocadownload/userupload/irmo/mc/2026/MC%20No.%2006%20s.%202026%20-%20Amendment%20to%20the%20Policies%20on%20Flexible%20Work%20Arrangements%20in%20the%20Government%20CSC%20Resolutions%20No.%202200209.pdf), and [OP MC No. 114, s. 2026](https://lawphil.net/executive/mc/mc2026/pdf/mc_114_2026.pdf)

### Overnight intervals and night-shift differential

The authorities use a 6:00 p.m.–6:00 a.m. next-day window for the differential, and the implementing circular gives examples such as a 10:00 p.m.–6:00 a.m. shift. That supports one interval with an end on the next calendar day. It does not create a second attendance domain: the interval is matched against the employee's effective Work Schedule Version, and only the qualifying portion is handed to a later payroll calculation. A break inside that interval is not qualifying night work. [RA No. 11701](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2022/ra_11701_2022.html), [CSC-DBM-GCG JC No. 1, s. 2023](https://www.csc.gov.ph/phocadownload/userupload/hrpso/CSC%20DBM%20GCG%20JC%20No.%201%20s.%202023.pdf)

The agency's authorization, applicability, and rate are evidence-bearing inputs. The product must not assume that every Appointed Personnel record is eligible for the differential, that every overnight schedule is lawful for the agency, or that the maximum rate is selected.

### Breaks

Lunch is excluded from the national 8-hour count. The 2026 CWW circular's one-hour lunch is an example of a compliant arrangement, not a universal break-length rule. Night-shift differential examples also exclude breaks, and OP MC No. 3, s. 2016 cautions against extended lunch or unauthorized non-work during official hours. [OP MC No. 3, s. 2016](https://lawphil.net/executive/mc/mc2016/mc_3_2016.html)

Accordingly, an agency schedule may carry explicit unpaid break intervals and their evidence. A break that crosses midnight is represented like any other cross-midnight interval. The expected-duty calculation subtracts only the breaks defined by the effective schedule; it must not invent a universal lunch or grace interval.

### Rest days, holidays, and work suspensions

The baseline excludes Saturdays, Sundays, and holidays from ordinary office days, but the flexible-work authorities permit weekends to be regular workdays with another compensatory rest day in the stated exigency cases. The 2026 CWW amendment also provides special treatment for a holiday or suspension falling on the identified nonworking day versus one falling within the CWW workdays. Therefore a date cannot be classified from its weekday alone.

Holiday data is a separate effective-dated concern. Proclamation No. 1006 supplies the 2026 national categories, but later proclamations (including dates dependent on the lunar calendar) can add or clarify dates. `HolidayCalendarVersion` should record date, category (regular, special non-working, special working, or suspension as applicable), scope, source, and supersession. A Work Schedule Version still controls whether the employee was expected to work on that date.

### Calculation unit, grace period, and rounding

The authorities use days, weeks, hours, and minutes for different purposes: the ordinary floor is 8 hours/40 hours; the night differential is an hourly rate; the implementing circular calculates an emergency fraction as minutes divided by 60; and the current CWW amendment refers to actual minutes/hours for late and undertime. None of those provisions authorizes rounding attendance timestamps to quarter-hours or a universal grace period.

Use exact whole-minute durations for raw attendance and expected duty. If a future agency rule expressly authorizes a tolerance or rounding convention, store it as an effective-dated agency rule and apply it only in the relevant calculation. Display-only formatting must not rewrite the underlying minutes.

## Attendance contract implications

The smallest model that preserves the cited rules is:

1. **Work Schedule Version:** agency, local timezone, effective range, weekly work/rest pattern, permitted flex/CWW/shift type, duty intervals, `end_next_calendar_day`, unpaid break intervals, expected minutes, coverage constraints, authority document, approving authority, and supersession. A version is immutable after use.
2. **Schedule Assignment:** Appointed Personnel, Work Schedule Version, effective range, and assignment authority. Prevent overlapping effective assignments for the same person; do not copy a new schedule into historical Attendance Days.
3. **Holiday Calendar Version:** scope, effective range, source proclamation/order, date, and holiday/suspension category. Resolve the version effective on the Work Date; do not hardcode annual dates.
4. **Attendance Event and Attendance Day:** retain raw event timestamps and correction history; resolve the effective assignment, schedule version, holiday version, expected duty intervals, and exact minute totals used for the calculation. For a cross-midnight interval, the beginning-local-date `work_date` is a project invariant, not a claim that national law defines that term.
5. **Approval boundary:** schedule/FWA adoption and schedule assignment carry agency authorization. Raw Attendance Events and Attendance Days remain calculable records; Attendance Correction is the approval workflow for changing a recorded fact or result. No cited source requires an additional approval layer for every ordinary attendance day.

This contract is deliberately fail-closed when a schedule, assignment, holiday scope, or agency authority is missing. It does not infer a night-shift entitlement, weekend work, holiday work, grace period, rounding, or special-regime rule from timestamps alone.

## Sources not converted into defaults

- No source located defines a government-wide grace period for arrival or departure.
- No source located prescribes quarter-hour, 15-minute, or other timestamp rounding for ordinary attendance.
- No source defines the product terms `Work Schedule Version`, `Schedule Assignment`, `Work Date`, or `Attendance Day`; those are domain contracts and must remain explicit project choices.
- No source supports a default night-shift differential rate, universal overnight eligibility, or automatic overtime merely because an interval crosses midnight.
- No source requires per-day Attendance approval. Agency-head authorization belongs to the work arrangement and internal rules; correction approval belongs to Attendance Correction.
